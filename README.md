## 🎓 Academic Research

**[no-universal-ranking](https://github.com/nicohernandez02/no-universal-ranking)** — LSE MSc capstone. Where the correlates of life evaluation hold, and how far they travel — CRE-Mundlak + elastic net/XGBoost validation, multiplicity correction, Oster bounds. Gallup World Poll, 168 countries, 2006–2025.

**[polity-size-rd-reappraisal](https://github.com/nicohernandez02/polity-size-rd-reappraisal)** — Fuzzy RD replication of Narasimhan &amp; Weaver (2024, AER) on India's gram panchayat delimitation — placebo cutoffs, bandwidth sensitivity, and a discrepancy found between the paper's write-up and its own numbers.

## 📊 Applied Projects

**[finbert-volatility-signals](https://github.com/nicohernandez02/finbert-volatility-signals)** — Predicting extreme stock price movements using FinBERT sentiment analysis on financial news. PySpark pipeline for 2.4M articles + XGBoost models.

**[cinema-narrative-shift](https://github.com/nicohernandez02/cinema-narrative-shift)** — Did TikTok change cinema? SBERT + cosine similarity analysis of 746 movie subtitles (2003–2025), measuring narrative repetitiveness before vs. after 2018.

## 📚 Coursework &amp; Practice

**[exploring-world-happiness](https://github.com/nicohernandez02/exploring-world-happiness)** — Exploratory analysis of subjective wellbeing across 170+ countries and 15 years — NumPy, pandas, Matplotlib.
